Interaction Guide
The garden -- A walled orchard seen from the south. Fruit trees drop ripe fruit; two spiderwebs hang on tempting routes. The fly only knows what it smells, sees, tastes and touches.
Camera -- Drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom, right-drag or two-finger twist to orbit. F follows the fly, R resets the view. The camera never moves anything in the garden.
Views -- Close-up (C) orbits the fly: drag to circle it, scroll in to its brain. Fly's eyes (E) is first person from its head: drag to look around, scroll to widen the view; the inset shows its brain. Esc returns to the garden.
X-ray -- The fly is glass, with all 139,255 neurons at their FlyWire positions inside its head. A neuron flashes when it fires: blue sensory, purple central, red motor. X toggles it.
Observe -- Click a fruit, web or the fly to see ripeness, remaining food, how visible a web is, or what the fly is sensing.
Fruit -- Click the ground to drop a ripe fruit. A hungry fly follows its smell; only eating reduces hunger.
Web -- Click the ground to hang a web facing the fly; click a web to take it down. Webs scare the fly by how large, near and fast-approaching they look.
Touch / Air -- Click the fly to touch it (head, thorax, abdomen or leg). Drag anywhere to blow a gust; the arrow shows its direction.
Light / Temp -- Dim or darken the garden, or warm and cool it.
Inspect -- Live traces of senses, neural activity and motor output, an event timeline, and repeatable experiments with seeds.
